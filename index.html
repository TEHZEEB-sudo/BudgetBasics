BUDGETING BASICS

Understand Your Money

Before creating a budget, it is important to understand your income, expenses, needs, wants, and savings.

Income

Money received from an allowance, scholarship, part-time work, or any other source. It helps you manage your monthly budget.

Example: Rs. 20,000

Fixed Expenses

Expenses that usually stay the same every month and are easy to plan for.These expenses are usually regular and predictable.

Example: Transport

Variable Expenses

Expenses that can change from month to month,such as food and shopping.These expenses may increase or decrease each month.

Example: Shopping

Savings

A portion of your income that you save for future goals or unexpected needs. Saving money helps you prepare for future Jexpenses.

Example: Rs. 4,000

Needs vs Wants

Before every purchase, ask yourself:"Do i really need this, or do i simply want it?"

Needs

50-30-20 Budget Rule

A simple way to divide your monthly income

50%

Needs

Use 50% of your income for important expenses like food, transport and education.

30%

Wants

Use 30% for things you enjoy, such as shopping, entertainment and hobbies.

20%

Savings

Keep 20% of your income for savings and future goals.
